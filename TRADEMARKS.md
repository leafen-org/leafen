## What you may do without asking

- Run, copy and share unmodified Leafen under its name, including inside a
  company or for others, and say so ("we run Leafen").
- Say what your work does with Leafen, in plain words: "works with Leafen",
  "an app for Leafen", "imports into Leafen", "hosting for Leafen".
- Write about Leafen, teach it, review it, and show its logo in that writing.
- Keep the name and logo in a copy you changed only to build, package or fix
  it for a platform, as long as you say it's changed and what you changed.

## What needs permission

- Calling your own product, service or fork "Leafen", or a name or logo that
  can be mistaken for it. A fork that changes what Leafen does takes its own
  name and logo; keeping "based on Leafen" in its description is fine.
- A hosting offer, a one-click template or a store listing that presents
  itself under Leafen's name or logo. Official templates are listed in the
  repository; others say "for Leafen", not "Leafen".
- Using the logo in your product's interface, or selling things that carry it.
- Calling yourself an official, certified or authorized Leafen partner,
  distributor or build.
