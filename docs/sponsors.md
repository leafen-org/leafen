## How to back Leafen

Through GitHub Sponsors at
[github.com/sponsors/leafen-org](https://github.com/sponsors/leafen-org) — the
Sponsor button on the repository page. Leafen runs no hosted service of its
own, so sponsorship is a direct way to keep its development going.

| Tier | What it gets |
| --- | --- |
| $5 a month — Backer | Your name and one-line message in Leafen's sponsor list and on this page |
| $25 a month — Supporter | Your name and one-line message in Leafen's sponsor list and on this page |
| $10 once — Back Leafen once | Your name on this page and, for the month you back it, your name and one-line message in Leafen's sponsor list |
| $100 once — Back Leafen once, company | The same, for a company |
| $250 a month — Company | Your logo, name and one-line message in Leafen's sponsor list and on this page, and in the row at the bottom of Home in Leafen (top ten companies) |
| $1,000 a month — Company+ | The same as Company, placed first |

## How sponsors are thanked

- Inside Leafen, a sponsor list shows up to a hundred sponsors of every tier,
  largest monthly amount first: company logos, names and each sponsor's
  one-line message. The top ten companies also appear in a quiet row at the
  very bottom of Home, with a way to see them all. Both arrive in an early
  update after the first release, and a server's operator can turn them off
  (`LEAFEN_SPONSOR_CREDIT=off`), for a company's own workspace that names no
  one else.
- Everyone is also listed on this page, past the first hundred too. Private
  sponsorships are listed only if you ask.
- Nothing else in the app carries sponsors: no loading screen, no menus. The
  app's one ask is the workspace menu's "⭐ Support Leafen on GitHub" line,
  which opens the repository page in a new tab; once you press it, it doesn't
  show for you again, on any of your devices. `LEAFEN_SPONSOR_CREDIT=off`
  takes it away too.
- Your listing links to your GitHub profile rather than straight to a
  website, so readers stay safe. Your GitHub account's name and picture are
  used as your name and logo.
- Leafen makes the sponsor list from GitHub Sponsors and publishes it signed;
  a Leafen server only draws that list and is never connected to GitHub for
  it. Servers check the list once a day; a list's sponsors show for 45 days
  from the day it was issued, so a company that stopped sponsoring doesn't stay
  on a server that no longer updates. Nothing records who saw what, and a
  server sends nothing about its workspaces, accounts or use when it checks
  the list.

## Your one-line message

GitHub's sponsorship checkout has no place for it. After you sponsor, send a
one-line message (about 40 characters, no links or sales copy) to
hello@leafen.org from an address on your sponsoring GitHub account or your
company's domain. We look at it before it is listed and translate it into
Leafen's languages, so each reader sees it in their own. Until a message
comes, your line says when you started sponsoring ("Sponsoring since" the
month you began).

- A company's domain is one its GitHub organization verified — not a free
  mail domain (gmail.com, naver.com and the like), and not a shared platform
  anyone can have an address under (github.io, notion.site, vercel.app,
  linktr.ee and the like). An address on the sponsoring account may be free
  mail. Mail is taken when it passes its domain's DKIM or DMARC check.
- To change how you are listed, write to hello@leafen.org the same way.
- Not accepted: gambling, adult content, and competing Notion-like workspace
  products. Hosting companies are welcome.

## Short terms

- You license your name, logo and message for the listings above. What the
  message says is your responsibility; Leafen may translate it and shorten it
  to fit. The maintainers may decline or remove a listing that doesn't fit
  these rules.
- Sponsorship funds Leafen's development; a listing is a thank-you, with no
  guaranteed views. No refunds.
- Your amount and the month's total are not made public.
- The list is signed, published at least once a month, and bundled with each
  release for servers without the internet.
